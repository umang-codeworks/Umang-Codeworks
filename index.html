OUR STORY

Hand-folded momos, made for Kanpur

Momos Darbar is a neighbourhood momos stall in Barra, Kanpur. We fold every momo by hand and cook it when you order, so it reaches you hot. Steamed, fried or crunchy, each plate comes with chutneys and mayo on the side.

Drop by the stall, or order from home on WhatsApp.

Fresh ingredients

Vegetables, paneer and cheese corn fillings made fresh every day.

Hygienic preparation

Cooked at the counter and served on clean eco-friendly plates.

Many ways to enjoy

Steamed, fried, crunchy and special momos, plus spring rolls.
